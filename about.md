I am currently a Senior Backline Technical Solutions Engineer on the Databricks Platform team. In this role, I focus on solving complex and interesting customer challenges that arise during the implementation of Data Engineering, Data Warehousing, and AI/ML solutions on the Databricks Platform.
### More Information

I began my career as an Associate Software Engineer, supporting an Enterprise Data Catalog tool used for data lineage and profiling use cases. This on-premise solution relies on Hadoop and MongoDB to store lineage mapping information across customers’ data platforms. I held this role for approximately 1 year and 8 months.

I joined Databricks in 2021 as an Associate Technical Solutions Engineer (L3), where I built a strong foundation in cloud concepts, primarily on AWS and Azure. Over the next two years (2022–2023), I expanded my exposure to all three major cloud providers and developed deep expertise in the Databricks Platform.  
 
In 2022, I was promoted to Technical Solutions Engineer (L4), and in 2024, I advanced to Senior Technical Solutions Engineer (L5). I view the period from 2021 to 2024 as my foundational phase, during which I gained substantial knowledge of Databricks architecture and cloud platforms.  
 
In 2024, I transitioned to the Backline Platform team, where I focus on complex technical issues that customers face with the Databricks Platform and its integration with cloud platforms—typically cases where the Frontline Support team is unable to reach a resolution. In this role, I serve as a bridge between Support and the Databricks Engineering team, ensuring critical issues are thoroughly analysed and appropriately escalated. I also collaborate with engineering by highlighting key areas for product improvement to enhance the overall customer experience.
